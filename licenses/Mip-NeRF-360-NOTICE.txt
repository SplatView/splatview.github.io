Mip-NeRF 360 dataset attribution

The bundled Bench example is a reconstructed Gaussian scene supplied by the
SplatView app owner, who identifies its source data as Mip-NeRF 360.
The web app uses the same SOG bytes as the desktop tutorial scene.

Dataset authors: Jonathan T. Barron, Ben Mildenhall, Dor Verbin,
Pratul P. Srinivasan and Peter Hedman.
